// 08 — faq

Frequently asked questions

What does a Business Central technical consultant do?

A Business Central technical consultant designs and builds the technical side of a Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central solution: AL extensions, integrations with other systems, data migration, reports, upgrades from Dynamics NAV and performance troubleshooting — turning business requirements into upgrade-safe code.

Can you upgrade Microsoft Dynamics NAV to Business Central?

Yes. I have upgraded and migrated solutions from Dynamics NAV 2009 R2 and NAV 2015 to modern Business Central versions, including converting C/AL customisations into AL extensions, upgrading data and migrating reports. Read my NAV to Business Central upgrade guide.

Can you integrate Business Central with a .NET application?

Yes. I build ASP.NET Core APIs, background workers and Azure Functions that talk to Business Central through its standard and custom REST/OData APIs using OAuth 2.0 service-to-service authentication.

Do you work with clients outside Nepal?

Yes. I work remotely from Kathmandu and have delivered Business Central projects for retail and duty-free businesses in Bahrain, the Maldives and the wider Gulf region.

Which Business Central versions do you support?

Business Central online (SaaS) and on-premises, plus legacy Dynamics NAV versions from NAV 2009 R2 onwards when an upgrade or maintenance is needed.

How do we start a project?

Send a short description of your requirement through the contact form or email diwas668@gmail.com. I will reply within one business day to schedule a discovery call and agree on scope, timeline and cost.
